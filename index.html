How it works

Booking Sendy takes about a minute.

Five stages, in this order, every time — so you always know what's happening next.

  1. 01

    Tell us

    Message us on WhatsApp with what you need, where it's coming from and where it's going.

  2. 02

    Agree the price

    We come back within minutes with a final figure. Nothing moves until you've said yes to it.

  3. 03

    We assign a rider

    A verified rider near the pickup is matched to your errand, and we tell you who it is.

  4. 04

    We handle it

    Shopping, pickup or drop-off — your rider keeps you posted, including if something's unavailable.

  5. 05

    Done, and paid

    You get your items, pay by cash or transfer on completion, and rate how it went.

About Sendy

Life is already busy. Your errands shouldn't make it harder.

Sendy Errands started with a simple observation: the small tasks — a market run, a package pickup, grabbing lunch — take up a disproportionate amount of time and energy. So we built a service where you say what you need, and a real, verified rider handles it.

Mission

Give people back the time and energy that everyday errands quietly take away — handled by someone reliable, not left to chance.

Vision

To be the first name that comes to mind whenever an errand needs doing, in every city we serve.

Values

Trust, speed and honesty. We'd rather tell you an item is unavailable than pretend everything went perfectly.

Questions

Good to know before you book

What kind of errands does Sendy handle?

Market shopping, grocery runs, food and package pickups, deliveries and personal errands. If it doesn't fit a category, just describe it — we'll tell you if we can do it.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp, or call. Tell us what you need, where it's coming from and where it's going. There's no account to create and nothing to install.

How much does it cost?

Errands start from ₦1,000 to ₦1,500 depending on the type, then vary with distance and what's involved. We confirm the exact figure with you before a rider moves.

How do I pay?

Cash or bank transfer, arranged directly with us once your rider is on the way. There's nothing to pay online.

Can I follow my errand?

Yes. Every errand gets a reference and a clear status, and we message you as it moves — including if something goes wrong.

What if something goes wrong with my order?

Reach us on WhatsApp or by phone and a real person will sort it out, refund requests included. We'd rather fix it than argue about it.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Ikotun-Igando and cover a growing part of Lagos. Coverage is expanding — ask us and we'll confirm whether we reach you before you commit.

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Messages sent outside those hours get answered when we open.
